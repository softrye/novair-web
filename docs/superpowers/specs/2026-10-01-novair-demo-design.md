# NOVAIR Demo E-commerce Design

## Goal
Create a presentation-ready optical e-commerce website for NOVAIR, structurally inspired by the current GMO Peru storefront while using only NOVAIR branding, demo products, and original visual assets.

## Brand constraints
- Palette: #1F304A, #C9DAE1, #F3EBE3, #FFFFFF, #20242B.
- Corporate font: Inter.
- Use the official NOVAIR logo/isotype assets; do not rebuild the logotype.
- Digital tone: clear, spacious, trustworthy, elegant, young.
- White or ivory base surfaces, NOVAIR blue for primary actions, mist blue for support surfaces.
- UI radii 12–18 px; pill controls may use 999 px; base spacing multiple of 8 px.

## Structural reference
Follow GMO Peru's storefront information architecture without copying its brand assets or proprietary copy:
- promo strip
- primary header with search/account/favorites/cart affordances
- category navigation / mega-menu feel
- hero campaign banner
- trust/benefit strip
- category discovery
- best-seller carousels/grids
- editorial promo blocks
- guided purchase steps
- highlighted collections
- newsletter/contact/footer

## Demo scope
- Static frontend only; no checkout, payment, user accounts, inventory, or backend.
- Demo catalog of at least 12 invented NOVAIR products with prices in soles.
- Sections for oftálmicos, sol, blue-light/office, kids and sale.
- Search/filter interaction and quick product modal.
- WhatsApp demo CTA using a placeholder number.
- Responsive mobile navigation.

## Success criteria
- Clearly reads as NOVAIR, not GMO.
- Layout rhythm is recognizably based on GMO Peru's e-commerce structure.
- Desktop and mobile screenshots are presentation-ready.
- No copied GMO product names, brands, logos, or marketing assets.
